About us

Tradition with a refined modern presentation.

Diwyashakthi Wedamedura is an Eppawala-based Ayurveda destination focused on traditional knowledge, professional communication and a customer-friendly experience.

Our story

A place built around trust, heritage and wellbeing.

Our approach respects Sri Lanka’s traditional Ayurvedic heritage while making information, communication and access simpler for today’s customers.

We aim to present authentic Ayurveda clearly and professionally, with visual language inspired by herbs, traditional preparation, heritage materials and the original Diwyashakthi green-and-gold identity.

What matters to us

Respect for tradition

We present Ayurveda with cultural respect and a focus on responsible wellness communication.

Customer comfort

Clear information and approachable communication are central to the experience.

Accessible information

Phone, WhatsApp, Google Maps and social channels make it easier to reach the Eppawala centre.

Professional presentation

Our digital identity now follows the original Diwyashakthi green-and-gold brand palette with a premium Ayurveda aesthetic.
